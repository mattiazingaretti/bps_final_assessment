Executive Summary

Background

DTV, a leading Technology, Media & Telecommunications company, is preparing for its first Regional Internal Audit. This report analyzes the "Subscribing to a new Language Course" process within the Training & Development department, identifying critical issues and providing actionable improvement recommendations.

Scope of Work

  • Process mapping and documentation
  • Issue identification and risk assessment
  • Improvement opportunity analysis
  • Action plan development with timeline

Key Findings

Critical 🔐

Data Security Vulnerability

Sensitive employee data stored on public Google Drive with unrestricted access.

High 📊

Inconsistent Evaluation

No standardized criteria for determining reimbursement eligibility.

Medium 📋

Undocumented Process

Process relies on tribal knowledge with no formal documentation.

Medium ⚙️

Manual Legacy Systems

Outdated Excel files and paper-based reimbursement forms.
