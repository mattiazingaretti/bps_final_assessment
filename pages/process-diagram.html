BPMN Flowchart: Subscribing to a New Language Course

1.1 Process Overview

The "Subscribing to a new Language Course" process enables DTV employees to request enrollment in English language courses to enhance their professional skills. The process involves multiple stakeholders and includes approval workflows, progress monitoring, and reimbursement decisions.

1.4 Decision Points Analysis

DP-001 🔀

Manager Approval

First-level approval based on employee's role and responsibilities.

YES: Forward to HRBP with justification
NO: Notify employee with rejection reasons
DP-002 🔀

Talent Management Approval

Second-level review considering daily tasks and role alignment.

YES: Register employee and coordinate enrollment
NO: Notify employee and manager with reasons
DP-003 🔀

Objectives Achievement

Evaluation of progress based on TOEIC results and attendance.

YES: Approve reimbursement, send to Finance
NO: Create action plan, suspend reimbursement
CONTROL GAP ⚠️

Inconsistent Criteria

No standardized criteria for DP-003. Some analysts use fortnightly meetings, others rely solely on TOEIC scores.

Audit Risk: Subjective decisions leading to unfair treatment

1.5 Documented Assumptions

1 Process Scope

  • • Process applies uniformly across all regions (Latin America, Western Europe, US)
  • • Only English language courses are covered by this process

2 Stakeholders

  • • Each employee has a designated Manager/Director
  • • HRBP is available for each business area
  • • Single Language Institute provider

3 Timeline

  • • Approval process takes 5-10 business days
  • • Progress reports received every 6 months
  • • TOEIC exam required for course completion

4 Financial

  • • Reimbursement is conditional on objectives achievement
  • • Employee pays upfront, company reimburses upon completion

5 Data Management

  • • Excel file "English Students 2012" is the single source of truth
  • • Google Drive storage with current access settings

6 Communications

  • • All formal communications via email
  • • Physical forms for reimbursement requests
