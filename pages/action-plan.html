Action Plan Proposal

Data Security Remediation Project - 12 Week Implementation

3.1 Executive Summary

Objective

Migrate sensitive employee data from unsecured Google Drive storage to an enterprise-grade HRIS with role-based access control, ensuring GDPR compliance and audit readiness for the upcoming Regional Internal Audit.

Scope

In Scope

  • • Language course data
  • • Employee records
  • • Progress reports
  • • Access controls
  • • User training

Out of Scope

  • • Other HR processes
  • • Payroll system
  • • Other training types
  • • Legacy data archival

Key Metrics

12
Weeks Duration
€100K
Budget
27
Activities
6
Phases

Success Criteria

  • 100% data migrated to secure platform
  • Zero unauthorized access incidents
  • GDPR compliance certification
  • Audit passed without findings
